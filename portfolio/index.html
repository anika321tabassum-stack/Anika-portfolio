Collection & Development — 2025

The Clay Muse

Rooted in heritage, The Clay Muse honors traditional hand-molded terracotta clay dolls (Tepa Putul) by blending rural folklore and indigenous craftsmanship with modern design. Defined by raw clay textures, warm earth tones, and primitive hand-pinched silhouettes, this collection reimagines historic folk art into a story-driven, contemporary creative narrative.
